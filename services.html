Services

What We Do

No matter what service I provide, I am grounded in practical, real world solutions. For 30 years I have been working in nonprofits that deliver services 365 days a year no matter the challenges. I cut through consultant speak to actionable solutions customized to your needs and capabilities.

Core Services

Strategy, Operations & Governance

Organizational Assessment

Your programs address the essential needs of the community; they should be as effective and efficient as possible. I can help you understand where bottlenecks are forming, help you meet industry standards, and implement best practices. I'll take you from assessment to implementation for better operations.

Strategic Planning

I provide in-depth strategic planning tailored to nonprofit organizations. Effective organizations define clear goals, develop actionable roadmaps, and align organizational resources for sustainable growth. Clients receive strategic documents and implementation guidelines to advance their mission.

Board Development

Good governance is the baseline for effective nonprofit organizations. I will help your board understand their roles and responsibilities to create positive relationships with the staff and community. Your board should be your biggest advocates — I help them focus on how they can help your organization grow.

Specialized Services

Leadership & Specialized Support

Executive Succession Planning

Executive succession is vital to the continued success of your organization. Our Executive Succession Planning services provide a structured approach that not only mitigates risks but also positions your nonprofit for sustained growth. Make your executive transition a launching pad, not a burden.

Interim CEO / Executive Director

My Interim CEO/Executive Director service offers expert leadership for nonprofits, leveraging over 25 years of experience to navigate transitions smoothly. I conduct a comprehensive organizational assessment to identify strengths and areas for growth, ensuring your mission remains on track. We also provide executive search services to find permanent leaders who align with your vision.

Animal Welfare Operations

I've spent more than 25 years running animal welfare organizations — from municipal animal control and high-volume urban shelters to regional humane societies with veterinary clinics and community programs. That experience lets me get to work on day one, whether the challenge is intake flow, length of stay, spay/neuter programs, or municipal contracts. I ensure your organization follows HSUS, ASPCA, and ASV best practices and builds practical policies your team can actually use.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What kind of nonprofits do you work with?

I work with nonprofits of all sizes, from small community organizations to large regional agencies. My background is deepest in animal welfare but I bring value to any mission-driven organization.

How do we know what services we need?

We'll start with a conversation about your organization's current state and goals. From there I can recommend which services would be most impactful.

What does your process look like?

Every engagement starts with a listening phase — understanding your mission, your team, and your challenges. Then we move to assessment, then to co-creating actionable solutions you'll actually use.

Do you work remotely or on site?

Both. I work across the United States and can deliver most services remotely, with on-site visits when the work calls for it.

What makes you different?

I'm not a traditional consultant who delivers a report and disappears. My goal is to leave you with practical tools and increased capacity, not dependency.

Do you work with staff or boards?

Both. Many of my engagements involve working with the entire leadership team — executive staff and board — to align strategy and strengthen governance.
